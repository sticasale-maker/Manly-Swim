How swell gets into the bay

Why a south-easterly barely reaches the corner while an easterly runs straight in

Cabbage Tree Bay opens to the north-east, and Shelly headland guards its south-east side. A swell from the east or north-east has open water all the way to the bay mouth. A south-easterly has to swing round the headland to get in.

The direction next to the swell height matters as much as the height itself.
