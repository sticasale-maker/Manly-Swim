South Steyne thefts

Swimmers’ citizen reporting
South Steyne, Cabbage Tree Bay and Fairy Bower — Manly, NSW
Data collected and provided by
Reports are lodged by swimmers through the Manly Swim app. Records are insert-only and server-timestamped; the reporter can add a police event number or a recovery outcome afterwards, and can change nothing else. Happy to make the underlying records or a live export available on request.

    How to read this pack

      Where the bags were taken

      Each pin is one report, numbered the same way throughout this pack. The shading darkens where reports fall on the same spot. Pins are placed by the reporter on the app's own map of the bay at the time of lodging.

      One reportSeveral, same spotThe wallPins on the same spot are nudged apart; the hairline points to where it happened.

      When

      A theft cannot be placed at a moment — only inside the window between the bag being put down and being found gone. The windows are drawn individually below; the charts count the hour the bag was put down unless the toolbar switch is on.
